### Author checklist

- [ ] Aggregates only; no learner-level data.
- [ ] `n`, spread, and missing data are visible.
- [ ] No claim that the organisation is "secure" or compliant.
- [ ] No prediction of incident reduction without evidence.
- [ ] One action with an owner and a review date.
- [ ] Limitations are next to the claims.
- [ ] Data-sharing terms and retention are stated.
