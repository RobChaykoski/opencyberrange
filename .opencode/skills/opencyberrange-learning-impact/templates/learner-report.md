### Author checklist

- [ ] Two strengths, each with evidence.
- [ ] One next step, with a cadence and a success criterion.
- [ ] No diagnosis, label, or public rank.
- [ ] No comparison to named peers.

- [ ] Accommodations are described and do not lower the score.
- [ ] The learner has a correction route.
