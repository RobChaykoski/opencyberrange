### Goal quality check

- Is the goal institutional, not an activity count?
- Is the baseline from the same definition and cohort?
- Is the target achievable within the remaining weeks?
- Does the owner have the authority to act?
- Is there a signal that will show whether the change worked?
- Is the sample size enough to compare?
