each party.

### Student agreement

- The goal is mine.
- The next practice is specific and feasible.
- I know who to ask for help.
- I know when we will review it.

## One-next-step rule

Every review ends with:

- one next step for the student;
- one next step for the instructor;
- one next step for the programme.

If there are more than three, the review is not finished.
