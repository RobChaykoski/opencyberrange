vx-underground notice

vx-underground is a public malware research archive and source-code
collection. The collection declares no licence for its samples, and the
material is live malware.

OpenCyberRange therefore treats vx-underground as reference-only. It does not
download, copy, execute, mirror, or serve a vx-underground sample, and it does
not place a sample hash or binary in this repository.

Cite the archive and the original family or author when using its published
research or metadata.

Source: https://vx-underground.org/
