ICS/OT PCAP FORENSICS CASE #OT-2026-031
======================================

SCENARIO
  A utility's incident-response team recovered packet captures from a safety
  instrumented system and a substation. The captures are real public research
  datasets, packaged offline for this exercise. Nothing here needs a live
  network, and nothing may be replayed onto a real control system.

GUIDE
  http://guide.lab
  The guide is a trimmed copy of the ICS-PCAP-Challenges range. It has 17
  guided challenges for the captures on this workstation, with objectives,
  hints, and self-check walkthroughs.

EVIDENCE

  /home/analyst/data/netresec-industroyer2/Industroyer2-StationAddress*.pcap

  Each dataset keeps its own LICENSE-NOTICE.md and PROVENANCE.md.

TOOLS
  tshark, tcpdump, python3, jq, standard Linux text tools. The guide's
  Pcapper track is not installed; use the tshark / Wireshark track.

OBJECTIVES
  1. Inventory the three incident captures and identify their protocols.
  2. Reconstruct the TRITON/TRISIS TriStation session against the SIS.
  3. Reconstruct the Industroyer2 IEC-104 command traffic.
  4. Compare normal and injected GOOSE traffic in the substation capture.
  5. Record detection guidance for each technique.

FINAL FLAG
  Build the flag from the TRITON capture:
    OCR{<engineering-workstation-ip>_<TriStation-port>_<frames-on-that-port>}
  Example shape only: OCR{10.0.0.1_1234_42}

NOTES
  - Captures are read-only evidence. Do not modify them.
  - Real ICS malware traffic is present. Never replay it, and treat any
    extracted file as live malware: hash it, do not run it.
  - Work offline. The workstation has no route to a real control network.
