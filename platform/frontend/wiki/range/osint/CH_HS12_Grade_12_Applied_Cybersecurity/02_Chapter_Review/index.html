    What You Practised

    The Grade 9–12 Pathway

    Grade Focus Example skill
    9 Digital safety foundations Recognise a phishing message and protect an account
    10 Systems and networks Explain addresses, DNS, and how a web request travels
    11 Defensive security Read a log and describe what happened

    Reflection

    1. Which part of the DevFest activity required the most careful reading?
    2. What evidence did you record, and where did it come from?
    3. What would you remove from the slide before publishing it?
    4. How would you explain the risk to someone who has never heard of metadata?
    5. What is one rule you will use before sharing a school project online?

    Responsible Sharing

    • Do not publish a classmate's name, email, photo, or schedule without consent.
    • Remove metadata from files when it is not needed.
    • Ask before sharing a screenshot that contains someone else's account.
    • If you find personal information in a public file, tell a teacher or the event organiser privately.
