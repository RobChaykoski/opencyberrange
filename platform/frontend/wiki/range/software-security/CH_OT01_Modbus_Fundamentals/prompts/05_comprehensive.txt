# OT01-L5: Comprehensive OT Security Assessment — Prompt Questions (Planned)

## Multi-Vector Incident Scenario
1. You receive three correlated alerts:
   - Alert A: Modbus register 420 read on PLC-03 at 03:17 AM (off-hours)
   - Alert B: SNMP GET on the same PLC's MIB at 03:17 AM from an unknown IP
   - Alert C: OPC UA status change on the HMI at 03:18 AM
   Construct a timeline of what likely occurred. What additional evidence would you seek?

2. A plant-wide "process disturbance" is reported: motors running in reverse, valves cycling unexpectedly, alarm lights flashing. You discover that three PLCs have had their holding registers modified within a 2-minute window. Propose a forensic methodology to determine:
   - The attack vector (how did the attacker get in?)
   - The persistence mechanism (did they leave backdoors?)
   - The intent (what was the target — disruption, data theft, ransom, etc.)

## OT Incident Response
1. When an OT security incident is confirmed, what are the immediate steps to prevent further damage while preserving evidence?
2. How do you coordinate response between IT security teams and OT operations staff without causing plant downtime?
3. Under what circumstances would you take a PLC offline? What is the change management approval process?

## Regulatory & Compliance
1. If the incident involved modification of safety-critical setpoints, which regulations (OSHA, EPA, IEC 62443, NERC CIP, etc.) are triggered?
2. What documentation must be filed? Who must be notified (regulators, insurance, executives, customers)?
3. How does your incident response plan align with IEC 62443 security levels (SL 1–4)?

## Tabletop Exercise Design
1. Design a 90-minute tabletop exercise for a mid-sized water utility CISO and plant manager. What scenario would you use? What "injects" would you deliver?
2. What metrics would you track during the exercise (time-to-detect, time-to-contain, decision quality, cross-team communication)?
3. What after-action review (AAR) questions would you ask to improve the organization's OT security posture?

## Career & Skills Development
1. What OT security certifications are most valued (GICSP, CISSP-ISSAP, ISA/IEC 62443, etc.)?
2. How can a network security professional transition into OT security? What skills are transferable, what are new?
3. What online resources, communities, or conferences would you recommend for staying current in OT security?

## Final Exam / Capstone
1. Given a provided pcap of Modbus traffic + a simulated PLC image, identify:
   - All hidden flags (OCR{...}) embedded in register values
   - Evidence of unauthorized reads or writes
   - Indicators of compromise (IOCs)
   - Recommended immediate actions
2. Write a 500-word risk assessment for a small manufacturing firm considering deploying Modbus TCP devices without network segmentation or firewall protection.
3. Create a one-page "OT Security Quick Start" cheat sheet for a network admin who's never worked with industrial protocols before.
