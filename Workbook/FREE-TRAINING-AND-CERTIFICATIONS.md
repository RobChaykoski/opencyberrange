# Free training and certification next steps

These are optional, free external resources. They are **not** required to
complete a workbook chapter, and they do not replace the guided labs. Use them
when you want more practice on the same topic or a recognised next step.

Source: [SWOCTS Resources](https://www.swocts.com/resources), reviewed
**2026-11-08**. Free offers change; verify the current terms with the provider.

!!! warning "Keep learner data private"
    Do not post flags, learner names, screenshots of the platform, or course
    data on a third-party site. Use a personal account and the provider's own
    privacy settings. The platform's own privacy page explains what stays here.

## Free certification programs on the SWOCTS page

- **ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC)** — free training and exam through the
  One Million Certified in Cybersecurity initiative.
- **IBM SkillsBuild** — free courses and job-role learning paths, including
  cybersecurity.
- **Cato Networks SASE Expert Level 1** — SASE course and certification.
- **Palo Alto Networks Beacon** — free self-paced cybersecurity fundamentals
  courses; a free account is required.
- **Professor Messer CompTIA Security+** — free video course for Security+ exam
  preparation.

## How to use these without losing the thread

1. Finish the workbook chapter and its analysis questions first.
2. Pick **one** external resource that matches the same topic.
3. Write down one thing it explained differently from the lab.
4. Bring that difference back to the workbook review or your instructor.
5. Do not collect certificates instead of practising. The lab evidence is the
   point; a certificate is a signal, not the skill.
