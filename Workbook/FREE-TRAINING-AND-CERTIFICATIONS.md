complete a workbook chapter, and they do not replace the guided labs. Use them
when you want more practice on the same topic or a recognised next step.

Source: [SWOCTS Resources](https://www.swocts.com/resources), reviewed
**2026-11-08**. Free offers change; verify the current terms with the provider.

## Tools

- [Burp Suite Community Edition](https://portswigger.net/burp/communitydownload) -
  free proxy and repeater for authorised web testing. Install it from
  PortSwigger; it is not bundled with the platform.
- [OWASP ZAP](https://www.zaproxy.org/) - open-source web proxy and scanner.
- [Wireshark](https://www.wireshark.org/) and [Nmap](https://nmap.org/) - packet
  analysis and network discovery.
- [CyberChef](https://gchq.github.io/CyberChef/) - encoding and data
  transformation; the platform also serves a local copy in the Tools panel.
- [EXPLIOT](https://expliot.io/open-source-framework/) and
  [HomePWN](https://github.com/Telefonica/HomePWN) - third-party IoT and
  embedded testing frameworks. Check their licence, pin a reviewed version, and
  use them only on devices you own or have written permission to test. Do not
  connect an unreviewed tool to the platform lab network.

## How to use these without losing the thread

1. Finish the workbook chapter and its analysis questions first.
2. Pick **one** external resource that matches the same topic.
3. Write down one thing it explained differently from the lab.
4. Bring that difference back to the workbook review or your instructor.
5. Do not collect certificates instead of practising. The lab evidence is the
   point; a certificate is a signal, not the skill.
