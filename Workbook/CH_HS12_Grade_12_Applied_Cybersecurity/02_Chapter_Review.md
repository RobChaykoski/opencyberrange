## Responsible Sharing

- Do not publish a classmate's name, email, photo, or schedule without consent.
- Remove metadata from files when it is not needed.
- Ask before sharing a screenshot that contains someone else's account.
- If you find personal information in a public file, tell a teacher or the
  event organiser privately.
