# Impact & recognition

**Impact & recognition** answers a different question from your evidence: not *what can
you do*, but *what did you choose to do with it, and did it last?*

Capability and impact are separate on purpose. A lab flag proves you can do
something. A contribution proves that someone else was helped, taught, or
protected.

## The five components

| Component | Question |
|---|---|
| Build | What did you create? |
| Protect | What did you secure? |
| Teach | Who did you help learn? |
| Serve | Who benefited from your time? |
| Stewardship | Were you trusted? |

## Recording a contribution

1. Open **Impact & recognition**.
2. Choose the component and the type — for example Build → lab, or Teach →
   mentoring.
3. Write a short title and say what changed.
4. Add the number of people reached. For teaching, use learners who retained
   the material, not attendance.
5. Submit it for verification.

A contribution starts as **pending** and earns no score. Another person — an
instructor, a peer, or an external validator — must verify it and give it a
quality rating. Self-reported work is never full credit.

## How the score works

Your verified contributions use a published, sublinear formula:

```text
points = base × reach × quality × longevity × validation × originality
       × repeat weight × decay
```

The shape of the formula is the safeguard:

- reach grows logarithmically, so 1,000 users are worth four times one user,
  not a thousand times;
- repeated contributions of one kind diminish within a year;
- a quality rating below 3 out of 5 is not verified;
- unused work decays after two years;
- copied work is worth a quarter of original work.

You can see the factor breakdown on every verified contribution. A correction is
a new record; nothing is silently rewritten.

## Meaning Index

The score is beside a simpler count of who was affected: organizations
protected, learners mentored, people taught, open-source users, communities
supported, and years of sustained contribution. A large score with no people
affected is a warning sign, not a triumph.

## Stewardship

Stewardship is a trust record. It starts at 100 and is changed only by an
administrator with a stated reason and an evidence reference. Responsible
disclosure and long maintenance can raise it; plagiarism, dishonesty, malicious
conduct, or abandoning an adopted project reduce it.

Only you, your instructor, and the administrator who recorded an event can see
it. It is never published.

## Privacy

- You see only your own impact.
- An instructor sees the learners they own or staff, to verify contributions.
- An administrator sees the institution.
- Cohort totals are hidden below five learners.
- Impact never appears on a public profile or transcript without your explicit
  consent.

## What is not measured

Hours logged, streaks, attendance, screen time, and content volume are not
contributions. The platform does not reward presence.
