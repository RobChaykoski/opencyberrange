# My Evidence

**My evidence** lists your practice records and their review state. It is the
learner-side view of the evidence ledger.

## What appears here

Each card shows:

- the source type and source id;
- the rubric id and version;
- the status: collected, in review, accepted, or rejected;
- the collection time and confidence;
- the written answer when the evidence came from a workbook;
- the competencies it supports;
- the latest instructor decision and next step;
- any appeals you have submitted.

Automatically verified flags and knowledge checks appear as **accepted**.
Workbook answers wait as **collected** or **in review** until an instructor
decides.

## Provisional competency mapping

If the lab's competency contract is pending, overdue, stale, or changes-
requested, the card says that the skills credited are provisional. The evidence
record, its review state, and its score are unchanged. The notice means the
competency mapping may change after subject-matter review; it is not a rejection
and it does not remove the record.

## Request a second review

If a record is **rejected**, a **Request second review** form appears on the card:

1. Explain what the reviewer may have missed. Point to the evidence rather than
   restating the answer.
2. Choose **Request second review**.

You can have one pending appeal per evidence record. The instructor sees the
appeal in the review queue and either accepts it, returning the record to
review, or rejects it with notes. An accepted appeal does not change the score
by itself; a reviewer must score the record again.

## Privacy and limits

- You see only your own evidence.
- An instructor sees evidence for learners in the courses they own or staff.
- An administrator sees the institution.
- An appeal is not a grade change and does not guarantee acceptance.
- Do not put passwords, flags, keys, or another person's data in an appeal.
