# Practice exams

**Practice exams** is the standalone drill for certification pathways. It is
separate from the short check that follows an exercise.

Open **Practice exams** in the sidebar. You will see the reviewed pathways, your
best score for each one, and your recent attempts.

## Choose a mode

- **Start mock** samples questions across the pathway with a timer and a pass
  mark.
- **Drill domain** focuses on one domain. Choose the domain first.

The number of questions and the time limit come from the pathway configuration.
Each pathway in the reviewed bank declares its own `exam` block (currently
20 questions, 30 minutes, 70% pass), and the server clamps that to the questions
actually available. With today's bank, a Security+ mock is 14 questions, not a
pretend 90-question replica. The page shows the real count before you start.

## During an attempt

- Options are shuffled for each question.
- Use the question numbers to move around.
- The timer counts down. If it reaches zero, the attempt is submitted with the
  answers you have given.
- You can submit early.

The correct answer is not sent to your browser during the attempt.

## After submission

You see your score, the practice pass mark, and every question with:

- the option you chose;
- the correct option;
- the explanation;
- a public reference;
- the domain it belongs to.

A pass here is a **study signal**. It is not a certification, a credential, or a
guarantee, and it does not appear on a certificate.

## Spaced repetition

The next attempt prefers questions you have not seen, then questions you have
missed most often, then questions you have not seen for a while. The rule is
fixed and visible; there is no hidden difficulty model.

## Your progress

**Practice exams** shows your best percentage per pathway and your accuracy by
domain. Use the domain accuracy to decide what to drill next, not as a grade.

## Privacy

- Your attempts are visible only to you.
- An instructor does not see your practice attempts in a roster.
- Practice results do not change your course progress, evidence, or Legacy
  Score.
