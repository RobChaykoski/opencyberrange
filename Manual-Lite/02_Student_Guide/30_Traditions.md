# The traditions

Tools change. Traditions endure. A tradition is a durable direction of practice
that survives the tool of the moment.

You do not pick a tradition. The **Living Archive** infers a tradition from
verified contribution. You choose a mission; the archive notices the path.

| Tradition | Archetype | Practice |
|---|---|---|
| Protection | The Guardians | incident response, SOC, threat hunting, detection, forensics, crisis response |
| Creation | The Builders | software, automation, DevSecOps, cloud, security engineering, tools |
| Discovery | The Cartographers | OSINT, research, intelligence, analysis, reverse engineering |
| Stewardship | The Keepers | governance, risk, documentation, maintenance, continuity |
| Illumination | The Lighthouses | teaching, mentoring, curriculum, speaking, technical writing |
| Connection | The Bridgekeepers | community, events, facilitation, partnerships |
| Service | The Citizens | outreach, volunteering, non-profit and public support |
| Judgment | The Wardens | architecture, threat modelling, risk and privacy decisions |

## Why traditions instead of job titles

A job title is a place a tradition is applied. It changes with the market. A
tradition describes what you carry forward: protecting, building, discovering,
keeping, teaching, connecting, serving, or judging.

That is why the platform does not ask "which job do you want?" as its first
question. It asks what you want to become part of.

## Traditions and journeys

Each journey belongs to one tradition:

- **Protection** — Defend communities, Protect healthcare, Secure critical
  infrastructure.
- **Discovery** — Hunt adversaries, Conduct research.
- **Creation** — Build security tools, Start a cybersecurity company.
- **Illumination** — Teach future defenders.
- **Judgment** — Support law enforcement.
- **Service** — Secure democracy.

If a tradition has no journey that fits, say so. The catalogue grows from what
learners and instructors actually need.

## Historical markers

Recognition in this platform is a **historical marker**, not a trophy. A marker
records what a generation built, protected, taught, connected, served, or
maintained. Markers never change your Legacy Score or your permissions.

Some markers show a clue. Some show only `????`. One or two show nothing until
they are earned. That is intentional.
