# Celebration Wall

The Celebration Wall is a shared recognition page for public wins from across
the range. It is not a leaderboard, a grade, or a ranking. It exists so learners
can appreciate their own achievements and celebrate a classmate's win.

## What appears on the wall

- Recent badges and achievements from learners who chose to be listed.
- The badge gallery, showing how many people earned and celebrated each kind of
  badge.
- Your own corner: your badge count, the celebrations your wins received, and
  your latest wins.
- A section for the badges people are celebrating. It is anonymous applause, not
  a popularity ranking.

Administrator accounts are not listed on the public wall. A learner in privacy
mode or with the wall listing turned off still sees their own corner but does
not appear in the shared feed.

## Celebrate a win

1. Open **Celebration Wall** from the sidebar.
2. Find a recent win in **Fresh wins**.
3. Select **Celebrate**.
4. The count increases and the button reads **Celebrated**.

One person can celebrate the same win once. Select the button again to remove
your celebration. You cannot celebrate your own win; your own rows show how many
people celebrated it.

## Privacy

- Celebrations are **anonymous**. The wall shows the count, never the person who
  celebrated.
- Celebrating is **voluntary**. You do not have to use it.
- The platform stores your account ID only to prevent duplicate celebrations. It
  is not shown to other learners.
- Celebrations do not affect progress, completion, certificates, course health,
  Cyber DNA, or any learning metric.
- Unlisting yourself from the wall stops new celebrations on your wins. It does
  not remove achievements you already earned.
- Administrator accounts are not listed.

## Change how your name appears

Open **Your corner** on the wall and expand **Privacy and name**:

- **Show me on the wall** turns your public listing on or off.
- **Name on the wall** lets you choose your real name, your username, or
  **Anonymous participant**.

The preference applies to the wall and to public recognition surfaces. It does
not change your private profile or your own corner.

## What the wall is not

- It is not a course scoreboard or a public rank.
- It is not a chat or messaging system.
- It is not a measure of learning, capability, or job readiness.
- It is not a place to share flags, credentials, personal data, or screenshots.
