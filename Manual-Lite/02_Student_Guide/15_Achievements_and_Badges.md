# Achievements and Badges

Earn achievements for how you solve labs inside a course and how far you explore: solving first, solving without hints, solving on your first attempt, and reaching learning milestones. Achievements are course scoped; the icons shown as badges on the scoreboard are these same achievements.

## Prerequisites

- Enrollment in a course. See [Joining a Course](12_Joining_a_Course.md).

## Where achievements appear

Achievements show in three places:

- The **Achievements** tab, which lists each earned achievement with an icon and label.
- As badge icons on each row of the **Scoreboard** tab.
- On your **Profile**, where earned course achievements appear with your certificates and completion record.

## Opening the Achievements tab

1. Open the course from **Courses**.
2. Click the **Achievements** tab.

**What you should see:** a list of achievements you have earned in the course, each with an icon and a short label. Before you earn any, the tab reads "No achievements earned yet in this course."

## Built-in achievements

| Achievement | How you earn it |
|-------------|-----------------|
| First Blood | First student in the course to solve a lab |
| Self-Reliant | Solve a lab without requesting any hints |
| Perfectionist | Solve a lab on your first attempt |
| Speed Demon | Solve a lab in under half its estimated time |
| Clean Sweep | Solve every lab in the course |
| On a Roll | Solve three or more in a row with no hints or on first attempt |

## Learning-path achievements

The platform ships with a prepopulated learning ladder. These are defined as
custom achievements, so an administrator can edit, deactivate, or add more
under **Admin -> Settings -> Achievements**.

| Achievement | How you earn it |
|-------------|-----------------|
| 👋 Hello, World | Complete your first exercise |
| 🔎 Curious by Default | Complete 3 exercises |
| 🧭 Terminal Tourist | Complete 5 exercises |
| 📡 Packet Whisperer | Complete 8 exercises |
| 🐚 Shell We Dance? | Complete 10 exercises |
| 🌱 Root of the Problem | Complete 15 exercises |
| 🐭 Lab Rat | Complete 20 exercises |
| 🏔️ The Long Game | Complete 25 exercises |
| 🐺 Lone Wolf | Complete 3 exercises without hints |
| 📚 RTFM | Complete 5 exercises without hints |
| 🧠 Hacker Ethos | Complete 8 exercises without hints |

!!! tip "They are a ladder, not a leaderboard"
    The milestones reward curiosity, practice, and understanding. They do not
    require speed, and they are not a measure of whether someone belongs in
    cybersecurity.

## Learning-time badges

Separate from the course ladder, the platform tracks time spent on exercises
and awards four range-wide badges. They appear on your profile under
**Across the range**, not inside a course card.

| Badge | How you earn it |
|-------|-----------------|
| Curious Mind | Spend at least 1 hour learning on exercises |
| Deep Dive | Spend at least 5 hours learning on exercises |
| Dedicated Learner | Spend at least 15 hours learning on exercises |
| Scholar | Spend at least 30 hours learning on exercises |

Time is counted once per learner and exercise. A timed completion is preferred;
otherwise the platform uses the bounded session windows so an abandoned tab
does not inflate the total. The platform checks these badges at sign-in and
after a correct flag, and backfills any badge you already qualified for.

## Hidden and secret badges

Some badges are intentionally hidden, including the RangeBox, Forensics, and
Software Security discovery badges. They are not shown on your profile by
default. To reveal the ones you have earned:

1. Open **Profile**.
2. Find the **Course achievements** section.
3. Click **Show secret badges**.
4. Click **Hide secret badges** to put them away again.

The preference is stored on your account. It affects only what your own profile
shows; it does not change whether a badge is awarded.

## Course scoped badges

Most achievements are earned inside a course. Solving a lab outside any course
still awards global badges, but course-scoped badges need a course enrolment.
Every achievement you earn is collected on your profile. See [Course Labs and Assignments](13_Course_Labs_and_Assignments.md).

!!! note "Awarding can lag"
    The platform checks for new achievements right after you submit a correct flag. The check runs in the background, so a badge can take a moment to appear on the tab.

!!! tip "Protect your bonuses"
    Self-Reliant and the points bonus for no hints both depend on solving without help. Requesting a hint forfeits them for that lab. See [Using Hints](08_Using_Hints.md).

To see how achievements feed into your standing, open [Viewing the Scoreboard](14_Viewing_the_Scoreboard.md).
