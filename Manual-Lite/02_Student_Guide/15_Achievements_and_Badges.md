# Achievements and Badges

Earn achievements for how you solve labs inside a course and how far you explore: solving first, solving without hints, solving on your first attempt, and reaching learning milestones. Achievements are course scoped; your profile and the Celebration Wall show what you have earned.

## Prerequisites

- Enrollment in a course. See [Joining a Course](12_Joining_a_Course.md).

## Where achievements appear

Achievements show in two places:

- On your **Profile**, where earned course achievements appear with your certificates and completion record.
- On the **Celebration Wall**, when you choose to be listed. The wall shows public wins from across the range; it is not a ranking.

## Finding your achievements

1. Open **Profile**.
2. Find the **Course achievements** section.

**What you should see:** a list of achievements you have earned, each with an icon and a short label. Course-scoped achievements appear alongside your certificates and completion record.

## Tiers, points, and the sampled quiz ladder

Every badge has a **tier** and a **difficulty**, and the two set its point value.
Bronze is a first step, Silver is solid practice, Gold is sustained or
higher-difficulty work, and Platinum is a standout achievement. A Platinum badge
on an expert exercise is worth more than a Bronze badge on a starter exercise.

Knowledge checks are optional, short, and tied to the exercise you just finished.
Each attempt draws **three questions** from the certification pathway that best
matches the exercise. The questions are original practice written to public
certification objectives, not copied exam items. The pathway can be CompTIA
Security+, CySA+, PenTest+, Network+, Linux+, Microsoft SC-900, ISC2 CC, OWASP
Top 10, or the OWASP LLM Top 10.

The sampled-quiz ladder is unique to SWOCTS:

| Badge | Tier | How you earn it |
|---|---|---|
| Quiz Ace | Silver | Score 100% on any exercise knowledge check |
| Platinum Check | Platinum | Answer all three sampled questions correctly |
| Relevance Ace | Platinum | Earn a perfect sampled check on an advanced or expert exercise |
| Hat Trick | Gold | Earn perfect sampled checks on three different exercises |
| Five in a Row | Silver | Pass five exercise knowledge checks in a row |
| Tier Climb | Gold | Climb from below 70% to a perfect sampled check |
| Quiz Polymath | Platinum | Earn perfect sampled checks across three different tracks |

After you submit, you see the correct answer, the explanation, and a public
reference for each question. Use the reference to check the source, then try
another randomised set. The quiz never gates lab access or a certificate.

## Learning-time badges

Separate from the course ladder, the platform tracks time spent on exercises
and awards four range-wide badges. They appear on your profile under
**Across the range**, not inside a course card.

| Badge | How you earn it |
|-------|-----------------|
| Curious Mind | Spend at least 1 hour learning on exercises |
| Deep Dive | Spend at least 5 hours learning on exercises |
| Dedicated Learner | Spend at least 15 hours learning on exercises |
| Scholar | Spend at least 30 hours learning on exercises |

Time is counted once per learner and exercise. A timed completion is preferred;
otherwise the platform uses the bounded session windows so an abandoned tab
does not inflate the total. The platform checks these badges at sign-in and
after a correct flag, and backfills any badge you already qualified for.

## Hidden and secret badges

Some badges are intentionally hidden, including the RangeBox, Forensics, and
Software Security discovery badges. They are not shown on your profile by
default. To reveal the ones you have earned:

1. Open **Profile**.
2. Find the **Course achievements** section.
3. Click **Show secret badges**.
4. Click **Hide secret badges** to put them away again.

The preference is stored on your account. It affects only what your own profile
shows; it does not change whether a badge is awarded.

## Course scoped badges

Most achievements are earned inside a course. Solving a lab outside any course
still awards global badges, but course-scoped badges need a course enrolment.
Every achievement you earn is collected on your profile. See [Course Labs and Assignments](13_Course_Labs_and_Assignments.md).

## Celebrate a classmate

The Celebration Wall lets signed-in users celebrate a public win anonymously.
Celebrations are voluntary and private:

- one anonymous tap per person per win;
- only the count is shown, never the celebrator's name;
- celebrations do not affect progress, completion, course health, or any
  learning metric;
- your own wins show how many people celebrated them;
- unlisting yourself from the wall stops new celebrations on your wins.

See [Celebration Wall](20_Celebration_Wall.md).

!!! note "Awarding can lag"
    The platform checks for new achievements right after you submit a correct flag. The check runs in the background, so a badge can take a moment to appear on your profile.

!!! tip "Protect your bonuses"
    Self-Reliant and the points bonus for no hints both depend on solving without help. Requesting a hint forfeits them for that lab. See [Using Hints](08_Using_Hints.md).

To see how achievements fit into your course progress and feedback, open
