# Recognition

Recognition tells you that the community noticed **how you behaved**: curiosity,
integrity, mentorship, service, persistence, and collaboration.

Recognition is not currency. An achievement never adds to your Legacy Score,
never unlocks a permission, and never appears in a ranking.

## Earned and unearned

On **Your impact** you will see:

- **Earned** — the title, category, and short message.
- **Clues** — some achievements show only a clue before they are earned.
- **Sealed** — the number of achievements you cannot see at all yet.

Secret achievements show a cryptic clue, not the title and not the criteria.
Legendary achievements show `????`. A sealed achievement shows nothing until it
is earned. There are no progress bars for secret achievements.

## What triggers recognition

Recognition reads **verified** contributions, never raw activity. Things you
did, attended, or clicked on your own are not enough. Time spent, points earned,
lab farming, and attendance never trigger an award.

Some recognition is awarded automatically from verified contribution. Some is
recorded by an administrator after a human confirmation — a workshop run, a
study group started, a learner welcomed. A manual award needs an evidence
reference and a note, and it is audited.

## If you think an award is wrong

Recognition is not a grade. If you believe an award was recorded in error,
contact your instructor with the date and the activity. A stewardship penalty
has its own appeal path through the administrator who recorded it.

## Privacy

- You see your own earned and unearned recognition.
- Secret achievements stay private unless you choose to share them in a later
  release.
- Recognition is excluded from public transcripts and verification pages until
  you opt in.
- Under-18 learners are excluded from public recognition surfaces.
- Achievements are never used as a grade, a prerequisite, or a disciplinary
  signal.

## A note on the hidden ones

You are not meant to find every achievement by grinding. Some are found by
behaving in ways that reflect the community you want to belong to. If you hear
"there is an achievement for that?", the answer is usually yes.
