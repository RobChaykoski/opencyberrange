# Workbook Answers

Workbook answers are short written analyst notes for an exercise. They capture
what you observed, how you know it, what you concluded, and what you would do
next. Saving an answer sends it to an instructor for review.

## Open the page

Choose **Workbook answers** under **Academy** in the left navigation. The page
loads the exercises that have a workbook page and are available to your account.

## Save an answer

1. Select an exercise.
2. Write at least 10 characters in **Your answer**.
3. Choose **Save and submit for review**.

The first save creates a new evidence record. If you edit the answer and save
again, a new version is added; the earlier version is not rewritten. That keeps
the review history honest if an instructor has already read the first version.

The page shows the last saved time. A successful save reports that the answer
was sent for instructor review.

## What the instructor sees

Your instructor sees the submitted answer text, the exercise, the competencies
from the exercise contract, and the **analyst-answer** rubric. The rubric covers:

- accuracy;
- evidence and provenance;
- analysis and conclusions;
- communication.

The instructor records a decision: accept, request more evidence, or reject with
a next step. The reviewed result appears in your evidence history and can
support your competency coverage.

## Limits and privacy

- Do not paste passwords, flags, API keys, VPN keys, or another person's data
  into an answer.
- A workbook answer is learner data. It is visible to you, your instructor for a
  course you are enrolled in, and an administrator. It is not public.
- An answer is not a grade by itself. It becomes evidence only when an instructor
  reviews it.
- Editing an answer does not erase the version an instructor already reviewed.
