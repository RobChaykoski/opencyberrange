# Knowledge Checks and Level Quizzes

OpenCyberRange has two quiz-like checkpoints. They look similar, but they are
not the same assessment.

| | Exercise knowledge check | Level quiz |
|---|---|---|
| Where | **Knowledge check** on a lab or from the dashboard | **Quiz** on a completed level |
| Purpose | Optional benchmark and practice | Scored check for the level certificate |
| Appears on a certificate | No | Yes, when you pass |
| Can you retry | Yes, as often as you like | Yes, the best score is kept |

Both use the same navigation and submission layout so you do not have to learn a
second pattern.

## Move between questions

- Use the numbered buttons at the top to jump to any question. A filled number
  means you have answered that question.
- Use **Previous question** and **Next question** below the question to move one
  question at a time. These buttons only navigate; they do not submit.
- The line between the buttons shows where you are and how many questions you
  have answered.
- On the last question, a reminder points you to the submission panel.

## Submit the quiz

The submission panel is separate from the navigation buttons.

- The exercise knowledge check button is **Submit knowledge check**. It scores
  the attempt, shows the explanations, and lets you try another set.
- The level quiz button is **Submit quiz for certificate**. It records the score
  used for the level certificate.
- If questions are unanswered, the panel says how many. Unanswered questions are
  marked incorrect.
- The button asks for confirmation before it ends the attempt. Choose **Keep
  working** if you want to review your answers.

## After you submit

The exercise knowledge check shows your score, each correct answer, your answer
where it differs, the explanation, and any reference. It remains a benchmark and
does not change lab access, course completion, or certificates.

The level quiz shows the score and pass state. When you pass, the result can
appear on the level certificate. A retry keeps the best score.

## On a phone

The numbered buttons, navigation, and submission panel stack into one column.
The question text and options stay full width. Use the browser's normal scrolling
to reach the submission panel.
