# Viewing Course Health and Feedback

The course workspace gives you a private, descriptive view of how a course is
landing. It replaces the old course scoreboard: there is no public ranking,
points table, or student league table.

## Prerequisites

- You own the course, or you are an administrator. See
  [03_Managing_Course_Settings.md](03_Managing_Course_Settings.md).
- At least one student is enrolled, so the course has a cohort.

## Open the course workspace

1. In the left sidebar, select **My Courses**.
2. On the **My Courses** tab, click the course title.
3. Use the course tabs.

## What each tab shows

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Course health: overall feedback, satisfaction, instructor engagement, response rate, knowledge checks, student voice, and the next action. |
| **Content** | The assigned modules and exercises. |
| **Feedback** | Student check-ins, aggregate clarity and challenge, anonymous comments, and the coaching list. |
| **Achievements** | Course-scoped achievements and celebrations. |
| **Analytics** | Completion, hints, attempts, time, abandonment, exercise signals, and the skill-tree coverage radar. |

## Read course health

The Overview uses a small set of private signals:

- **Overall feedback** — a weighted 0–100 summary of the available feedback.
  It is not a grade.
- **Student satisfaction** — experience ratings from the optional knowledge
  checks.
- **Instructor engagement** — coaching coverage and notes.
- **Response rate** — unique respondents divided by enrolled students.
- **Knowledge checks** — participation and average best score.
- **Student voice** — self-reported feeling from the course check-in, with n.

Use the status and the next action to decide what to change. A red status is a
prompt, not a verdict about a student or an instructor. Small cohorts are shown
as counts.

## Coach a student

1. Open the **Feedback** tab.
2. Select a student in the coaching list.
3. Read the feeling/doing pair: feeling is self-reported from the check-in;
   doing is progress. Neither is a grade.
4. Add an optional rating and a short next step.
5. Save the coaching note.

The note is visible to you and that student. It is not published on the wall or
in any ranking.

## Resetting a lab

If a score or completion looks wrong after you reset a student's lab, the reset
removes that lab's contribution and revokes course-wide achievements. See
[11_Resetting_a_Student_Lab.md](11_Resetting_a_Student_Lab.md).
