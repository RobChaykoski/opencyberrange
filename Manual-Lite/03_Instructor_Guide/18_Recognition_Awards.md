# Recognition awards

Recognition is the values-based layer above the evidence ledger. It records
integrity, mentorship, service, curiosity, and durable contribution. It is
separate from course micro-credentials and separate from the Legacy Score.

## Verify a contribution

A learner submits a contribution from **Your impact**. It arrives as pending.

1. Open the pending contribution in the review surface.
2. Check the claim against the evidence. A link, a repository, a workshop
   record, or the learner's own evidence record is enough to start.
3. Rate the quality from 3 to 5. A rating below 3 cannot be verified; reject it
   and say what is missing.
4. Set the number of people reached. For teaching, use learners who retained
   the material, not attendance.
5. Choose the validation level: peer, instructor, industry, or adoption. Only an
   administrator can record industry or adoption.
6. Verify or reject with notes.

Verification is not a rubber stamp. The score grows with reach, quality,
longevity, and validation, and it decays when work is unused.

## Award recognition manually

Some recognition needs a human signal the platform does not collect yet:
welcoming new members, making an introduction, starting a study group, helping a
non-profit. Record it as an administrator:

1. Confirm the activity and collect a reference — a thread, a ticket, an event
   record, or a partner confirmation.
2. Award the recognition key to the learner.
3. Write a note of at least ten characters.
4. The action is written to the audit log.

A manual award is never inferred from clicks. Integrity recognition is awarded
for self-reporting and responsible disclosure, never from suspicion.

## Stewardship

Stewardship starts at 100 and is changed only by an administrator, with a
reason and an evidence reference. Negative events require the reference.

| Band | Range |
|---|---|
| Trusted | 90–150 |
| Established | 70–89 |
| Watch | 40–69 |
| Restricted | below 40 |

A negative event is private to the learner, the instructor, and the recording
administrator. It is never published and never used as an automated accusation.

## Privacy and fairness

- Instructors see learners in courses they own or staff.
- Cohort aggregates are suppressed below five learners.
- Secret achievement triggers are never returned by the API before they are
  earned.
- Recognition does not change a grade, a prerequisite, or a permission.
- Do not award recognition for attendance, streak length, or content volume.
