# Participant brief

## What this is

You are helping test a cybersecurity teaching platform. We are testing the
platform, not you. There are no wrong answers and no penalty for getting stuck.

## What you will do

Starting from a browser with no account, you will:

1. create an account;
2. join a short course with an invite code;
3. connect the VPN if the exercise asks for it;
4. start the first lab;
5. submit the flag.

We will time the path and take notes on where you hesitate. The target is under
15 minutes.

## What to expect

- Think aloud: say what you are looking at and what you are trying.
- The facilitator will not help unless you are stuck for a while.
- The first help is a neutral question; later help is recorded as assistance.
- You can stop at any time.
- You can ask to have your notes deleted.

## Privacy

- We record a participant id, timings, device/browser, and short notes.
- We do not record your name, email, face, or voice unless you separately
  agree.
- A minor needs guardian consent and a facilitator present.
- Raw notes are kept for 90 days, then deleted; only aggregate results remain.

## Safety

Every lab target is fictional and isolated. Do not use a real credential or a
real system. If you see something that looks like a real secret, stop and tell
the facilitator.
