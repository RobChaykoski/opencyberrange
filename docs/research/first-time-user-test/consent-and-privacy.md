# Consent and privacy

This is an operational usability test. If results will be generalized as
research, use the [REB-ready survey package](../REB-SURVEY-PACKAGE.md) and obtain
approval before recruiting.

## Adult consent text

> I agree to take part in a usability test of the SWOCTS Cyber Range. I
> understand that the facilitator will observe me, time the onboarding path,
> and take notes about where I get stuck. The notes use a participant id, not my
> name. I understand that I can stop at any time and ask for my notes to be
> deleted. I understand that this is not a test of my ability, and that no
> employment, academic, or certification decision will be made from it.

## What is collected

| Data | Purpose | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| Participant id | link the timing sheet and notes | 90 days |
| Persona | compare first-time paths | aggregate only |
| Timestamps | measure the four phases and total | 90 days |
| Device, OS, browser | explain a failure | 90 days |
| Short quotes and stuck phase | find friction | 90 days |
| Consent form | prove consent | study close + 1 year |

Not collected by default: name, email, IP address, student number, face, voice,
screen recording, passwords, tokens, or learner rows.

## Storage and access

- Raw notes and consent forms stay on the encrypted test drive.
- Only the facilitator and the study owner can access them.
- Raw notes are deleted after 90 days; the aggregate report remains.
- A quote is used only with the participant id, never a name.
- A minor's data is never used in marketing or a public case study.

## Withdrawal

A participant can withdraw at any time. Delete their timing row and notes on
request, and record the withdrawal in the study log. Do not include their data
in the analysis.

## Incidents

If a real secret, a real person's data, or a live target appears, stop the
session, preserve only what is needed to fix it, and tell the privacy contact
(`privacy@swocts.com`). Do not paste it into a ticket or a chat.
