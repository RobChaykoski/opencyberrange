# Personas

Recruit outside the project team. A builder of the flow is not a first-time
user.

## Software developer

- Uses a terminal, Git, and APIs daily; new to cyber ranges.
- Expected friction: VPN client, course invite, lab network model.
- Watch for: assuming the lab is a normal VM, skipping the brief, expecting a
  package manager.
- Lab: `linux-1-1-ssh-service-detection`.

## General IT technician

- Windows/desktop support; some networking; little Linux.
- Expected friction: WireGuard install/import, terminal, flag format.
- Watch for: looking for a download button, trying RDP, not recognizing the
  target hostname.
- Lab: `linux-1-1-ssh-service-detection`.

## College student

- Digital native; limited networking; motivated by the course.
- Expected friction: registration rules, VPN, first terminal command.
- Watch for: password rules, approval wait, not knowing what a flag is.
- Lab: `linux-1-1-ssh-service-detection`.

## High school student

- Grades 9-12; minor-safe stream; guardian consent and facilitator present.
- Expected friction: account rules, browser lab, reading the brief.
- Watch for: age gate, privacy acknowledgment, no live target.
- Lab: a High School lab from the minor-safe stream. Do not use a live OSINT
  extension or a real person.

## Security professional

- Pentester or analyst; knows the tools, not this platform.
- Expected friction: finding the course, VPN profile, lab start.
- Watch for: trying the target before reading the brief, expecting a different
  VPN flow, judging the lab quality instead of the onboarding.
- Lab: `linux-1-1-ssh-service-detection`.

## Sample

Pilot with one participant per persona (5). Baseline with three per persona
(15), or as many as recruitment allows. Report the sample size per persona; do
not pool them to hide a weak one.
